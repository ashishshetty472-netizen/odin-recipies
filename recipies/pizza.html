Pizza

Ingredients:

  • 1 (12 ounce) package pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 475 degrees F (245 degrees C).
  2. Roll out pizza dough on a lightly floured surface.
  3. Transfer dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  4. Sprinkle with pizza sauce and top with mozzarella cheese.
  5. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, or until crust is golden brown and cheese is bubbly.
