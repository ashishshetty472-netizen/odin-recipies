Spaghetti

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound spaghetti
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions:

  1. Cook spaghetti according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add garlic and sauté for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in crushed tomatoes, oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Toss the cooked spaghetti with the tomato sauce.
  5. Sprinkle with grated Parmesan cheese before serving.
